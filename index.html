About This Library

About iWriteHub

iWriteHub is an independent learning library about how content gets found, understood and chosen on the modern web. Search no longer ends at a blue link: answers are read aloud, summarised by AI assistants and judged by the experience on the page itself. Our guides organise that reality into five layers — SEO, AEO, GEO, AIO and SXO — and teach each one with full, long-form articles instead of thin tips.

What we publish

Every article on iWriteHub is written for this site, in our own words, and structured the way we teach: a direct answer first, then the reasoning, the workflow, the tables and the checklists. We cover crawl budgets and canonical tags alongside featured-snippet writing, AI citation habits, entity clarity and honest conversion design, because in practice these are one connected system.

Our editorial standards

  • Original, always. We never copy another site's text. Where we describe a widely known concept, we explain it from first principles and our own examples.
  • No guaranteed rankings. Search engines do not sell positions, and neither do we. We teach durable methods, not tricks.
  • Evidence over folklore. Where a number appears (a pixel width, a word window), we explain what it measures and where its limits are.
  • Reader first. If a tactic would annoy a real visitor, we say so — even when it might squeeze a short-term metric.

The tools

The SEO Toolkit runs entirely in your browser. The readiness checker measures title pixel width with the same canvas text metrics your browser uses to draw text, counts answer words, and scores how closely a draft matches the ranges we teach in the AEO guides. Nothing you paste is uploaded, stored or tracked.
